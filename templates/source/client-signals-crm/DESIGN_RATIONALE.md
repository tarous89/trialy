# Design rationale — signal-first CRO CRM

The reference pattern is Attio's linked company / person / deal object model (not code, not images, not its private datasets). CRO-specific differentiation is source-backed public company intelligence and an explicit fact-versus-interpretation decision trail.

Rather than another repeated KPI dashboard, the app uses seven distinct task compositions:

1. **Signal-first sales command desk** — a single continuous ink-toned opportunity/signal mast, actionable follow-up list, stage progression rail and a compact high-priority account shelf.
2. **Company dossier** — searchable left-hand account index paired with a contextual identity card, linked professional people and reviewed source evidence; full register below for exact editing.
3. **Professional contact book** — selectable decision-maker list with a clearly visible opt-out/preference badge and a focused relationship card.
4. **Kanban deal room** — horizontal within a clearly bounded labeled region, followed by a selected-deal inspector and accessible paginated record register.
5. **Chronological evidence stream** — publication dates form a spine; fact and commercial interpretation have separate labels; review and source link are always visible.
6. **Research-scope notebook** — compact criteria form and text recipe with a dark research-control panel, explicitly disclaiming any task activation.
7. **Editorial account brief** — typographic source chronology, separately stated CRO hypothesis and reviewed report archive.

**Palette:** deep charcoal-teal `#112832`, warm near-white `#f6f8f4`, fresh lime `#ddf76c` for genuine signals, restrained blue `#478ab7` for procedural stages, and muted green/terracotta reserved for meaningful status. Not an imitation of Attio. No optional in-app TrialAgents branding, fake logo, or repeated enterprise marketing banners. The host supplies its official watermark and account navigation.

**Small-team constraints:** one title/subline per page; compact form controls; full app name always visible; single page-level vertical scroll; only labeled Kanban/table regions may scroll horizontally; record actions remain usable at mobile width. Buttons and all transient inputs have stable host event markers.
