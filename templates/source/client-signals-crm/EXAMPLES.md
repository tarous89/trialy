# CR06 example workflows

All names, facts, URLs and dates in `demo_data.json` are synthetic sample records intended only to illustrate structure and behavior.

## 1. New account from evidence

Open Company directory; propose a company with unique verified website, focus, country, employee estimate (only if sourced), owner and date. After reviewing/saving and receiving its real ID, create one or more professional contacts. Record any opt-out verbatim. A funding signal must have an original HTTPS source, published date, fact, review state, separate interpretation and duplicate check; it is never automatically a qualified opportunity.

## 2. Deal qualification

Create an opportunity linked to the company, give the CRO service need and owner, stage `Identified`, qualification `Pending`, next action and due date. After an actual BD discussion, submit a new dated stage rationale and explicitly mark `Confirmed` if justified. `RFP` or `Negotiation` never follows just from a high-relevance funding signal. No forecast is inferred from user-entered probability.

## 3. Human review of public signal

For a hypothetical new press release: store public URL + date + sourced fact, assign interpretation and reviewer separately, then propose review. Duplicate source/company/type/date proposals are rejected. A high-relevance signal with unknown budget remains a hypothesis. Choosing Consider deal preselects the account in a new **reviewed** opportunity form; it does not write a new deal automatically.

## 4. Account briefing

Choose a company, read authorized reviewed evidence, draft factual summary and explicitly stated commercial hypothesis. Cite signal ID/version, e.g. `s01@1`, exact source date, report window and reviewer/date. If a signal is revised, return briefing to Draft and revalidate current references. Partial evidence forbids reviewed status.

## 5. Research without automation

Use Signal monitoring to edit region, themes and company exclusions, inspect the generated research recipe, and run a manually authorized ChatGPT research session. Review source results, then propose approved records. Zero background jobs is a fully supported path.
