# Reference and originality audit

**Reference:** [Attio — Understanding objects](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-101/attios-data-model/understanding-objects), and [Understanding the data model](https://attio.com/help/reference/attio-101/attios-data-model/understanding-attio-data-model). Attio's people, companies, deals and relationships inform the logical structure; this source does not copy its artwork, screenshots, CSS, implementation or private customer records.

**Approved CR06 blueprint:** `docs/product/app-template-blueprints/CROS.md#cr-06` in `tarous89/trialy` (2026-11-01). The relevant design goal is client signals plus CRM for small CROs, with public professional company intelligence and optional ChatGPT research. Current template authoring/usage standards `1.3.9` in `templates/TEMPLATE_AUTHORING.md` and `templates/TEMPLATE_USAGE.md`.

**Synthetic demo:** All examples use fictional biotech, names, business development events and `example.org` URLs. They do not represent claimed real corporate funding, live PDUFA decisions, real contacts or trial enrollment. The demo does not leak into production setup by default.

**Exclusions:** No email provider, external CRM API, paid lead database, social site scraper, patient identifiers, implicit prospect opt-in or automated outbound communications. Research is only authorized when the user asks for it and the existing platform provides the verified tools.
