# Contracts: automations (1.1.0 to 2.0.0)

Contracts declares **no automations** in 1.1.x and 2.0.0 (owner decision, 14 November 2026). It has no install recommendations, no scheduled AI work and no emails. Overview, Obligations, CDAs and Pipeline show deadlines, obligations due, CDA confidentiality ends and agreements waiting for signature on demand. Filling in a contract is done by the user's connected Claude or ChatGPT when asked.

Users may add their own automations later in TrialAgents → Automations. The app never reads automation state.

Removed in 1.1.0: the 1.0.0 optional weekly digest and the daily extraction of new originals, plus the `digest_day` setting.
