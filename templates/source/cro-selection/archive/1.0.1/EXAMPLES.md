# Example requests

**Create a CRO selection for our EU Phase II oncology program, inviting four CROs and a five-criterion scorecard.**

Make a private app-linked selection with brief scope, 4 CRO organizations and explicit weighted criteria after review. No emails are sent or bids invented.

**Compare three received CRO proposals on price, start-up timeline, therapeutic fit, oversight quality and staffing.**

Use recorded bids and 1–5 cited ratings only; show missing answers, mandatory gates, score coverage and separate price figures. No automatic award.

**Draft a leadership CRO selection memo and notify internal reviewers when approved.**

Prepare evidence-based narrative from approved records; request human review. Optionally prepare a TrialAgents email automation only after saved decision, with authorized recipients.

**Create a CRO selection app with no automation.**

Save a usable app with inherited access and no runner, email or schedule dependency.

The demo_data.json file and preview.html contain fictional organizations and proposals only. Never install these records as customer data.
