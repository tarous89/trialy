# Fundraise 2.0.0: automations

Declared in `catalog.metadata.json` → `recommendedAutomations` under the [app automations scope](../../../docs/product/RECOMMENDED_AUTOMATIONS.md) (template guidance 1.3.27–1.3.30). Install recommendations are on by default (#176; `APP_AUTOMATION_RECIPES_ENABLED=false` is only an operator off switch). Both are selected in Add app; Add app is the one approval and enables them after the app is saved. The owner can turn either off there, or later in TrialAgents → Automations (From app features) or through Claude or ChatGPT with the exact review. The app stores no schedule, recipient or on/off setting, never reads automation state and keeps working when an automation is off, failing or missing.

| Key | Automation | Worker | Trigger | Reads | Saves / sends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `investor_research` | Weekly investor research | The user's Claude or ChatGPT runner | Calendar, Mondays 07:00 Europe/Berlin | Brief from the helper `research_brief`: fit vocabularies, planned and active rounds, every known investor (including rejected and archived) and investor organizations without a profile. Read bindings use `fields:"all"` (E6). | Stored in the app (#169), create only: new `organizations` (name, org_type, country, website), `fundraise.investor_profiles` (always `profile_status` proposed, with source and source_date; never tier, intro path or archived; at most 5 per run) and one `fundraise.research_reports` row per run, also when nothing new fits. No update grant; no pipeline, round or activity writes. Sends nothing itself. |
| `weekly_digest` | Weekly Fundraise digest | TrialAgents email | Calendar, Mondays 09:00 Europe/Berlin (two hours after research) | The installed helper `round_digest` (E7, `records.read` only): rounds, opportunities, activities, investor profiles, settings, research reports, organizations | One email to the owner. Round progress with the app's rules (raised = signed only; verbal and soft separate; other currencies flagged and excluded), the lead, follow-ups due this week (overdue first), stale conversations (`stale_days`), then **research this week** (the latest report from the last 7 days) and the proposed investors waiting for review. Partial reads withhold totals. Nothing to report (no active round and no research) → nothing is sent. |

**Why two automations and one email:** a single research-then-email automation (E5) would be neater, but on the current platform an email step after research whose text comes from an app helper does not deliver: the background check reads the research automation without the owner's database identity (row-level security hides it, `summary_unavailable`), and once that is fixed, each of the helper's reads re-validates the whole research configuration (about 3 s per read in the test database, so 8 reads exceed the 15 s helper limit). A calendar email at 09:00 avoids both, and still reports progress when no runner is connected.

**Not included:** a signed-commitment notice. Interest (including signed) is set by the user, who already knows; TrialAgents offers no e-signature that could change it.

**Without automations:** Overview lists stale and due conversations; Activity lists follow-ups due and pass reasons; Investors → Proposed is where research results (or the user's own proposals) are accepted or rejected, with the research runs listed below.

**Missing pieces leave the app usable:** without a ready runner or without write access, research stays at Needs setup (never proposal-only) and the digest still sends progress, saying no report was saved.

Tests: `tests/fundraise-automations.test.ts` (Add app selection, active setups, grants, schedules, the scheduler making research due, the brief, research writes and refusals, the delivered digest against the Overview screen and the saved report, no runner, archive pauses).
