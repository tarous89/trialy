# Market Radar 1.0.0 (`life.market-radar`)

Watches the market for one or more scopes: approvals, label changes, launches, HTA, pricing and reimbursement decisions, guidelines and reported sales, one row per company, product and indication. Sales are recorded as reported, per currency. Built on the [Bio Radars kit](../../radar-kit/README.md) 1.1.0; scope: [BIO_RADAR.md](../../../../docs/product/app-template-blueprints/apps/BIO_RADAR.md) §2, §3 and §5. **Draft:** not in `templates/catalog-seed.json` until the owner approves it.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `radar.json` | The definition: scope fields (indication, regions), tracker fields, natural key (company, product and indication), event kinds (approval, label_change, launch, hta_decision, pricing, guideline, sales_reported), digest section ("Approvals, HTA and pricing decisions"), standard sources (FDA, EMA, HTA bodies (NICE, G-BA, HAS), Company reports, Guideline societies), default research instruction, monthly cadence (first Monday, 07:00), Coming up window (180 days). Generated by `../_tools/make_wave3.py`. |
| `catalog.metadata.json` | Description, tags, whenToUse, whenNotToUse. |
| `demo_data.json` | Fictional scope, products, events and runs. |
| `schema.example.json`, `market-radar.preview.html` | Generated by `scripts/build-radar-templates.mjs`. |
| `QA.md` | §5 results. |

Roles: `organizations` (core.v1), `programs` (core.v1, optional, read-only), `marketradar.settings`, `.scopes`, `.products`, `.events` (output, `radar.v1`, one writer), `.runs`.

Automations (install dialog, both selected): **Market Radar scan + digest** (monthly) and **Market Radar high-impact alert**, as for every radar. Research may create and update products (except watched, seen_run_id, dismissed, user_notes, archived), create events and create runs; it never touches scopes or the template.
