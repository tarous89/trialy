Preserve existing IDs, disabled state, grants and hidden configuration when editing. Respect paused/disconnected setup. Prefer supported Work chat, high reasoning and the latest capable available model, reporting actual settings without inventing unsupported parameters.

Scope to the saved study and currently permitted app fields using inheritAppAccess:true. Read monitoring events, linked findings, approved agreement versions, obligation definitions and manually dated occurrences. Cite record IDs/versions, period and cutoff. Separate monitoring completion from PV on-time performance; disclose inaccessible, incomplete or unverified evidence. Never infer legal deadlines, clinical safety conclusions, compliance, future recurrences or patient information. Deduplicate by study, agreement/version, obligation, period and source version. One-time drafts stay in the authorized conversation. Recurring drafts default to private permission-checked monitoring history. No automatic business writes. An explicitly approved report destination must be the actual existing snapshots table with scoped expiring grants and Draft status, never invented IDs or overwrites of Reviewed evidence. Complete zero results is completed; no_update requires full coverage and an approved quiet policy. Partial/denied/expired/blocked runs do not advance coverage. Preserve checkpoints and receipts on retry.

Replace every placeholder from the saved app/table receipt, approved physical keys, original app version, expiry and current registered recipient access. Recipients are the owner and only explicitly chosen, registered, currently authorized teammates. Business contacts, investigators, CRO addresses and agreement counterparties are not platform recipients. Use fixed subject/body directing the owner to the app; no interpolated confidential record data, research-completion email, second schedule or extra action steps. The catalog metadata cannot create grants or activate delivery. Delivery failure never reruns research and cannot block manual app loading or erase drafts; inspect Logs and actual receipts.

This package enables no task, grant, schedule or email.
