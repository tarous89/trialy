# Data and evidence contract

Use schema_mapping.example.json exactly; equivalent schemas and mappings are included in catalog.metadata.json for MCP clients. All fields are scalar, including parent IDs. Preserve actual host record IDs and nonnegative versions. `versionTag` is the human document revision, distinct from host record version. Every projected row must include every mapped field: optional null is valid, missing authorization or a missing field is incomplete. Explicit archived booleans are mandatory for coverage. Never guess missing flags. Writes normalize optional blanks to null.

Reads are bounded to 80 rows × 12 pages for each of seven roles: at most 84 read calls and 960 rows/role. Totals must be known, nonnegative and stable; IDs and versions valid; pages nonduplicated, complete and scalar. Denied, unknown, missing, short or oversized sources withhold dependent readiness and safe writes. Complete authorized empty is distinct from unavailable. Narrow or revise source scope rather than treating truncation as all data.

Readiness requires at least one explicitly Required item, no Unknown applicability, rationale for every Not applicable item, and exact linked current Approved documents with dates/HTTPS source evidence. A document family has exactly one non-Superseded version. Exactly one active review event must establish Approved for that exact version, with reviewer, date and evidence; conflicting/pending decisions require reconciliation. Retain approved evidence; create document revisions and supersede old versions. Approved reviews and reviewed reports cannot be silently downgraded or archived.

Manually recorded Submitted/Authority follow-up/Closed phases require actual date and official receipt reference. They never perform a filing. The tracker distinguishes recorded historical phase from current evidence readiness. Questions require recorded response proof/date before Response recorded/Resolved. Original milestone baselines cannot change, and new target dates require a reason.

Reviewed report `sourceRefs` is comma-separated `role:recordId@vN`, with N >= 0, exact authorized version and same-submission linkage. Sources capture the pre-transaction evidence snapshot; the atomic parent version guard may advance its version during the review transaction. Preserve reviewed snapshots and use a new Draft revision for changed evidence. Duplicates reset review state and approval proof.

Child edits and archives are atomic batches with a same-status submission update pinned to the editor-open parent version; this rejects concurrent evidence changes across this app. Parent references, historical identities and dependent archives are checked from complete reads. Independent Database/API modifications are outside these application-level relationship guards and must be reconciled before relying on readiness.
