# Evidence and data contract

The exact ten scalar schemas and maps are in schema_mapping.example.json and in the MCP catalog. Roles: people, institutions, affiliations, experience, engagements, collaborations, actions, findings, requests, ratings. Field keys are snake_case; logical UI keys may be camelCase. Parent references are actual confirmed record IDs, never arrays, invented IDs or relationship objects. Optional dates, references, numbers and selects use null for empty. Host versions are nonnegative, including 0.

Every field must be authorized and projected, including optional fields. Explicit archived and optOut booleans are required; missing/denied access is incomplete. Reads require known stable totals, valid scalar rows, unique IDs and complete pages. Each of ten roles reads at most 9 pages of 80 rows (720 rows/role, 90 reads plus a proposal). Truncation, unknown totals, denied fields and short pages withhold scores/safe changes. Complete authorized empty differs from unavailable. Counts labelled loaded describe only the loaded rows.

A human defines three weights summing to 100: expertise, trial experience and geography. Each reviewed rating has three integer factors from 1–5. Score = sum(factor × weight)/5. No score without an active request, unique assessment for that investigator/request/revision, exact criteria revision, reviewer/date/rationale/HTTPS evidence, complete source reads, an active verified investigator with public identifier and explicit optOut:false, sourced registry experience and dated affiliation evidence. This is human-entered professional fit, not recruitment performance, availability, consent or patient access. Opt-out remains editable and immediately withholds comparison.

Reviewed ratings/findings cannot be silently downgraded or archived. Copied records reset protected identity/review fields. Active reviewed matching protects existing supporting profile/affiliation/experience evidence; close the study brief and create a new criteria revision before changing that evidence. Criteria used by reviewed ratings cannot be overwritten. Independent Database/API edits require reconciliation and new human review; these are app-level controls, not database-wide constraints.

Child proposals atomically include same-value direct-parent updates pinned to editor-open versions. Status is used for people/requests, name for other parents. This both requires those write scopes and advances parent record versions. Concurrent rating, criteria and supporting evidence edits are rejected rather than silently approved from stale snapshots. Parent/child relationships and archives require complete dependent reads. Follow-ups cannot reference another investigator's interaction. Archive preserves history; no hard deletes or external messages are issued.

No patient, private contact or proprietary licensed source data is supplied by this package. Fictional demo records are local test evidence only. Optional research produces private permission-checked drafts/history; reviewed database updates remain separate human decisions.
