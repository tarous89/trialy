6. 7. **Only mark Published after production verification**. A run may move to the next eligible topic if safe to do so, but may not claim a publication it did not verify. Never regenerate or overwrite an actively claimed article. 8. At the end of each run, provide a concise result: ID, title, live URL, git revision, verification, remaining total and any blocker. Avoid unsupported claims of one successful publication every hour; the schedule is hourly, and success depends on checks.
